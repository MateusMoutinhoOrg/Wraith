
## OBjetivo:
Implementar a mecanica de data de pagamento e data e transacao

## CLI and Task.yaml:

trocar **date** por due-date,payment-date e transaction-date.
~~~yaml
name: AddTransaction
account: your account
category: your category
amount: 0
transaction-date: date you made the trasaction  
due-date: the date its espected to be payed (defaults to transaction-date)
payment-date: the date it were payed 
description: null
apply: true
~~~

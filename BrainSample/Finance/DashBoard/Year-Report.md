## 5. 📚 What I've learned this year

1. **One client is not a business.** July proved it: one late payment, one negative month.
2. **Small daily habits cost the most.** R$ 10 a day on cigarettes is R$ 3,650 a year — more than the car loan interest.
3. **Saving first works.** Every month the transfer went out on payday ended positive.
4. **Paying the card in full every month** has cost me R$ 0 in interest all year. Keep it that way.

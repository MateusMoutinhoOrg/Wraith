## 7. 📚 Lessons from 2026

| # | Lesson                                                                      | Action for 2027                         |
| - | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 1 | **Prospecting stalled in May became zero income in July** (~2-month lag)    | Fixed target of 3 proposals/month, always |
| 2 | A single client is the biggest business risk, not spending                   | No client above 50% of income           |
| 3 | Daily micro-spending (`Vices`) added up to more than 1.5 months of rent      | Hard cap with automatic freeze          |
| 4 | Low fixed costs (29%) saved July from being a disaster                       | Keep fixed costs ≤ 30% of income        |
| 5 | Contributing "whatever is left" never happens — only scheduled ones work     | Auto-debit on the day income lands      |
| 6 | Reserve stuck at 3 months all year                                          | Reserve before any investment           |

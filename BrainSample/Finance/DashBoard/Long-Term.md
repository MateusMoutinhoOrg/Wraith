## 7. 🧭 Permanent Rules

1. **Reserve first.** No risky investment before 6 months of reserve.
2. **Expensive debt before any contribution.** Interest > 1%/mo takes priority over the market.
3. **Lifestyle cap:** expenses never exceed 60% of the average income of the last 12 months.
4. **Contributions are automatic**, on the day income lands, before any spending.
5. **No client above 50%** of revenue for more than 2 quarters.
6. **Plan review every 6 months**, recalibrating assumptions.
7. **Income increase → 70% to contributions**, 30% to quality of life.
